## Historical access statistic

The submission's Arkansas statistic refers to ATLAS 2019–2020 data: 152 of 303
secondary schools (50%) were listed without athletic trainer services, with
41 schools (14%) listed as unknown. It is historical statewide data, not a
current estimate for Arkansas District 3.

ATLAS project: https://koreystringer.institute.uconn.edu/atlas/
Source table recorded during development:
https://draft-koreystringer-institute.media.uconn.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/3814/2024/06/AT-Services-By-State-Tables-1-4_2019_2020.pdf
